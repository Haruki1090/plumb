# plumb v0.27.1 - ledger: prices for gpt-6.2-sol and gpt-6.2-astra

Rows of two Codex models had an unknown cost in `report`, `views` and `records`: `gpt-6.2-sol` and
`gpt-6.2-astra`.

## What changed for users

- `gpt-6.2-sol` is priced at $2 input, $0.10 cached input and $10 output per million tokens, and
  `gpt-6.2-astra` at $8, $0.40 and $40.
- These are the OpenAI API's standard-tier prices, read directly from OpenAI's pricing and model pages
  on 2026-11-18. A cache write is the version's 1.25x input, and above 272K input tokens the whole
  request takes the model's `long_context` tier (sol: $4 input, $0.20 cache read, $15 output; astra:
  $16, $0.80, $60). Batch, Flex, fast, ultrafast and regional tiers are not modelled.
- The two models are added to the existing version `2026-11-10`, not to a new one: they are new and
  their price has not changed since launch. No priced row changes and no `price_version` changes; the
  newly priced rows are what become known.
- `codex-auto-review` stays unpriced: OpenAI publishes no rate for it.
- `gpt-5.6-sol`'s promotional price now runs until 2026-11-30 per OpenAI's pricing page (version
  `2026-11-10` says at least through 2026-11-21). When it ends, a new version is needed once OpenAI
  publishes the standard rate.

## Verification

`scripts/test-ledger-cost.py` checks that version `2026-11-10` adds exactly `claude-fable-5-2`,
`gpt-6.2-sol` and `gpt-6.2-astra` to what `2026-11-09` carries, and prices a sol and an astra row on
2026-11-18 at the standard rates (including a 1.25x cache write), each with a row above 272K input
tokens at the long-context tier and one at exactly 272K that stays standard, all with `price_version`
`2026-11-10`.
