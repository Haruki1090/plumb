# plumb v0.22.2 - ledger: prices for terra, luna and sol 5.6

Rows of three Codex models had an unknown cost in `report`, `views` and `records`: `gpt-6.1-terra`,
`gpt-5.6-luna` and `gpt-5.6-sol`.

## What changed for users

- `gpt-6.1-terra` is priced at $1 input, $0.05 cached input and $5 output per million tokens,
  `gpt-5.6-luna` at $0.20, $0.02 and $1.20, and `gpt-5.6-sol` at $4, $0.40 and $20.
- These are the OpenAI API's standard-tier prices. A cache write is the version's 1.25x input, and
  above 272K input tokens the whole request takes the model's `long_context` tier, at 2x input and
  cache and 1.5x output. Batch, Flex, fast, ultrafast and regional tiers are not modelled.
- The prices are in a new version, `2026-11-09`, effective 2026-08-22. A new version is needed because
  luna's 80% cut on 2026-07-30 and sol 5.6's promotional price from 2026-08-21 (at least through
  2026-11-21) did not hold for the whole of the previous version. Luna's and sol 5.6's rows before
  2026-08-22 stay unknown.
- The new version copies every model of `2026-10-30` unchanged, so no existing priced row changes; the
  newly priced rows are what become known.

## Verification

`scripts/test-ledger-cost.py` checks that the new version carries every model of `2026-10-30` with
identical values, and prices a luna row on 2026-11-01 (including a 1.25x cache write and the tier above
272K input), keeps a luna row on 2026-08-01 unknown, and prices terra and sol 5.6 on 2026-11-01.
