# plumb v0.22.1 - ledger: prices for OpenCode Go

Rows of OpenCode Go's models had an unknown cost in `report`, `views` and `records`, so they were left
out of every total and every model comparison.

## What changed for users

- `opencode-go/deepseek-v4.1-flash` is priced at $0.15 input, $0.003 cache read and $0.60 output per
  million tokens, `opencode-go/deepseek-v4-pro` at $0.66, $0.022 and $1.98, and `opencode-go/glm-5.3`
  at $1.40, $0.26 and $4.40.
- These are Go's Off-Peak list prices. Go doubles them at Peak (01:00-04:00 and 06:00-10:00 UTC, Monday
  to Friday); the price table has no time-of-day tier, so Peak is not modelled. Go lists no cache-write
  price, so a cache write is priced at the version's 1.25x input. On the Go subscription none of this
  is the bill: like a flat-rate Claude plan, the cost compares work across models.
- The prices come from models.dev, OpenCode's own model catalog (`providers/opencode-go/models/` at
  commit 065c9d1), which transcribes Go's documentation page (accessed 2026-10-21). That page itself
  could not be opened from the session that made this change; each `source` says so.
- Other Go models are not priced yet. Kimi K3 lists a cache-write price of its own ($3, 1x input) that
  the version's 1.25x multiplier would not match, and it changed on 2026-10-23, so it needs a version
  of its own.

## Verification

`scripts/test-ledger-cost.py` checks the three models' listed rates, their `checked` and `source`, and
prices a DeepSeek V4.1 Flash row (1M input, 1M cache read, 1M output = $0.753).
