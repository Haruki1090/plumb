# plumb v0.15.1 — doctor is fast again

## What changed for users

- **`plumb-doctor` answers in about 20 seconds** (81 s in 0.15.0). It runs selftest in quick mode,
  which skips the slow suites with a visible `--` line; the full `scripts/selftest.sh` keeps them.

## Compatibility

No behaviour change outside doctor and selftest. `PLUMB_SELFTEST_QUICK=1` is the only new switch.

## Verification

`scripts/check-harness.sh` and `scripts/selftest.sh` (full and quick) pass. Timed on the cloud
image: doctor 18 s, quick selftest 15 s, full selftest 38 s.
