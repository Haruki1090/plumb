# plumb v0.24.0 - lead: lane labels and owner alerts through the pane driver

`plumb:lead` kept a lane's state (implementing, review round, checks, ready PR) only in its ledger, and
said what goes up to the owner only in the reply. With three or four lanes running, the owner had to open
the ledger to see where each one stood, and missed an escalation while looking at another pane.

## What changed for users

- `docs/role-map.md` has a new section, "Labels and alerts, when the driver has them". A `pane.driver`'s
  installed instructions may offer a label on a pane and an alert to the owner. The label is display
  only and never evidence; the ledger and the report file stay the record. An alert is for what the
  owner must decide or do, and one the driver reports as not shown is not retried.
- `plumb:lead` step 5 puts every ledger line for a lane on that lane's pane label when the driver has
  one.
- `plumb:lead` "What goes up" raises the driver's alert together with the escalation, and never for a
  lane that only finished.

## Do you need to do anything

No. A driver whose installed instructions say nothing about labels or alerts behaves exactly as before.
To get the new behaviour, describe the driver's label and alert commands in its own instructions (the
skill or file that `pane.driver` points at), outside this repository.
